# Business example assets

Six fictional business presentations are bundled for editing and demonstration: Aurelia (press announcement), Orbit (product launch), Northstar (quarterly results), Meridian (sales), Harbor (customer story), and Lumen (90-day strategy). Their companies, statements, and metrics are illustrative.

Each example includes four editable scenes, narrated audio clips, a music bed, one stock photograph, and a finished 1080p MP4. All files are local. The application makes a fresh editable copy for each browser workspace while sharing the immutable sample files.

The scene documents and original layouts are supplied under the application’s AGPL-3.0 licence. Bundled stock images are supplied under the **Pexels License**, not AGPL. See [stock-credits.json](stock-credits.json) for each photographer, original source page, and licence URL. Keep those credits with redistributed examples. Do not distribute the photographs as a competing stock collection or imply endorsements from depicted people.

Narration was generated with ElevenLabs v3; instrumental music was generated with Sonilo 1.1 through fal.ai for these demonstrations. The audio and rendered videos are supplied as example media, subject to applicable provider output terms and third-party rights. They are not licensed as reusable voice models. No voice clone or biometric training data is bundled.

Provider generation IDs, API keys, workspace cookies, and private account state are not needed to use these examples. New generation is optional and billed to the keys you add in Connections.

The manifest identifies the six document files. Media URLs use `/media/samples/<project>/...`; keep the `media/` directory when copying the examples.
